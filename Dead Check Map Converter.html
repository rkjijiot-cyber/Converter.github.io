Dead Check Map Converter

Turn a Gorebox .gbmap or a 3D model into a Dead Check map ZIP you can import with F8.

Contains the converter, a one-click .exe builder and a short guide.

Set it up

  1. Install Python from python.org. In the installer, tick “Add python.exe to PATH”.
  2. Unzip the download and double-click build_exe.bat. It installs what it needs and builds DCMapConverter.exe (internet needed once, a couple of minutes).
  3. Open DCMapConverter.exe, choose a map or model and press Convert. Import the ZIP it makes in Dead Check with F8.

What it reads

.gbmap (V1, V2 and JSON), .obj with its .mtl and textures, .glb, .gltf and .stl. For FBX or Blender files, export to .glb first.

Good to know

  • It was built without access to the game, so texture size, left/right mirroring and spawn height are best guesses.
  • Windows may warn about an unknown publisher. You build the .exe yourself from the included source.
  • It converts geometry only. Navigation and NPC behavior are not part of it.
  • Artwork in converted maps stays with its original owners.
